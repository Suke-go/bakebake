Event2026/11/19

We received the Best Poster Award at HAI 2026!

We took part in HAI 2026, held in Osaka, Japan, on November 16–19, 2026, and presented a poster on BAKEBAKE’s yōkai-generating apparatus. On November 19, the final day of the conference, the presentation received the Best Poster Award.

HAI, the International Conference on Human-Agent Interaction, explores how people and agents interact. This was its 14th edition.

A presence you sensed for a moment, or an unease that is hard to put into words: describe such an ambiguous experience, and the apparatus draws on traditional yōkai folklore to suggest a name, a story, and an appearance. By choosing and revising the suggestions, participants make their experience visible as a yōkai.

We are grateful to everyone who visited the poster and discussed the work with us. In December, we will bring the apparatus to SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

Award: HAI 2026 Best Poster Award

Paper title: BAKEBAKE: Making Ambiguous Experiences Visible through Generative Yōkai

Authors: Kosuke Shimizu, Riri Ikebe, Hiroki Ichikura, Mirai Hoshikawa
