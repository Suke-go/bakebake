Exhibition2026/02 – 2026/05

Exhibited at YOKAI EXPO 2026, Shodoshima

The revised apparatus was exhibited at YOKAI EXPO 2026, Japan's largest yōkai fair, held on February 22, 2026 at Tonosho Town Hall (Furetopia Hall) on Shodoshima, Kagawa. Kazuhiko Komatsu, the leading scholar of yōkai studies, tried it and gave an evaluation based on his expertise. Exhibitions continued at further venues; 79 sessions produced 66 yōkai.
