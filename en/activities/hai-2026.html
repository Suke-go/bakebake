BAKEBAKE in HAI 2026

We are presenting a paper on BAKEBAKE’s yōkai-generating apparatus at HAI 2026. HAI, the International Conference on Human-Agent Interaction, explores how people and agents interact. Its 14th edition takes place on November 16–19, 2026, in Osaka, Japan.

A presence you sensed for a moment, or an unease that is hard to put into words: describe such an ambiguous experience, and the apparatus draws on traditional yōkai folklore to suggest a name, a story, and an appearance. By choosing and revising the suggestions, participants make their experience visible as a yōkai.

Paper title: BAKEBAKE: Making Ambiguous Experiences Visible through Generative Yōkai

Authors: Kosuke Shimizu, Riri Ikebe, Hiroki Ichikura, Mirai Hoshikawa
