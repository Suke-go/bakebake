Hyakki Yagyō百鬼夜行

Hyakki Yagyō, the night parade of one hundred demons, is a game in which you gather yōkai from across Japan and call their parade to your own town. It is built on records from the Kaii-Yokai Folklore Database maintained by the International Research Center for Japanese Studies.

Choose a region and your town, then invite five yōkai from the clouds drifting over the map. Each of the five calls companions from other places that share the same story, and the gathered yōkai march to your town as a parade.

Tap any yōkai in the parade to read the folklore record behind it. Through play, you discover that similar stories have been handed down in places far apart.

Play Hyakki Yagyō (Japanese, bakemon.net/hyakki)
